# Sector Modules

## 1. Modular approach

The platform is designed around one shared set of capabilities with configurable sector workspaces. Each organisation should see the fields, indicators, workflows and report structures relevant to its work.

The modules are configurations of a common platform, not eight unrelated applications.

## 2. Initial demonstration sectors

The first demonstration will cover three sector configurations.

### A. Public Health Programmes

Possible programme areas:
- Community health education.
- Disease prevention and awareness campaigns.
- Immunisation outreach.
- Maternal and child health initiatives.
- Community screening and referral programmes.
- Health promotion and prevention activities.

Possible records and indicators:
- Communities or locations reached.
- Outreach sessions planned and completed.
- People reached, using clearly defined counting rules.
- Referrals made and followed up, where appropriate.
- Facilities or community groups engaged.
- Activities completed against plan.
- Evidence and reporting completeness.

The demonstration must use synthetic information. It must not present itself as a clinical system or use real patient records.

### B. Health Organisation Programmes

This configuration is intended for organisations that deliver, coordinate, support or evaluate health programmes.

Possible programme areas:
- Health service improvement projects.
- Facility support and capacity-building.
- Community health worker training.
- Health access initiatives.
- Health systems strengthening.
- Health research and programme implementation.

Possible records and indicators:
- Facilities supported.
- Training sessions and participants.
- Service-readiness activities.
- Programme activities completed.
- Communities served.
- Implementation milestones.
- Programme outputs and outcomes.

Health organisations may handle sensitive data. The design should default to collecting only the information required for programme monitoring. Patient-identifiable records are outside the demonstration scope.

### C. Education Programmes

Possible programme areas:
- School support initiatives.
- Literacy and numeracy programmes.
- Teacher training.
- Digital learning initiatives.
- Education access projects.
- School infrastructure and learning-resource programmes.
- Community education and awareness.

Possible records and indicators:
- Schools or learning centres supported.
- Teachers trained.
- Learners reached, with clear definitions.
- Learning sessions delivered.
- Attendance or participation measures where appropriate.
- Learning materials distributed.
- Activities completed against plan.
- Evidence of programme outputs and outcomes.

Education activities may also include public awareness, community learning and training delivered by organisations outside formal schools.

## 3. Future configurable sectors

The architecture should be extensible to:

### Youth Development
Skills training, mentorship, youth participation, entrepreneurship and employment-support programmes.

### Humanitarian Projects
Emergency response, relief distribution, community support, response monitoring and recovery activities.

### Community Development
Community infrastructure, local development, participation, service access and community-led initiatives.

### Environmental Programmes
Conservation, restoration, environmental education, waste management and climate-related community projects.

### Research and Advocacy
Research activities, evidence management, stakeholder engagement, campaigns, policy engagement and dissemination.

These are future configurations. Their inclusion in the product vision does not mean they will be built in the first demonstration.

## 4. Shared fields and configurable fields

Shared fields may include:
- Programme name and description.
- Objectives.
- Location or service area.
- Start and end dates.
- Activity name and status.
- Responsible team.
- Indicator name and unit.
- Target and actual value.
- Reporting period.
- Evidence source.
- Update date.

Configurable fields may include:
- Sector-specific indicators.
- Beneficiary or participant categories.
- Service or intervention types.
- Facility, school or community details.
- Sector-specific report sections.
- Custom activity classifications.

## 5. Configuration principle

Organisations should not be forced to use every sector module. An organisation selects the relevant sector configuration and adapts it to its programme structure.

The shared platform remains consistent while its terminology, forms, indicators and reports change to match the organisation's work.
