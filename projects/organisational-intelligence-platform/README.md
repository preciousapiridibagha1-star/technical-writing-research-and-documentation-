# AI-Powered Organisational Intelligence Platform

**Status:** Product concept and documentation in development. No complete MVP, production deployment, organisational partnership or field validation is claimed.

## Overview

A modular platform concept for organisations that need to bring programme information together, monitor activities and performance, understand their records, and prepare evidence-based reports.

The platform is intended to serve organisations across multiple sectors through one shared foundation and configurable sector-specific workspaces. It is not designed as a fundraising product. Fundraising and donor management are not the centre of the concept.

## Initial sector focus

The long-term architecture is intended to support:

1. Public health programmes.
2. Health organisation programmes.
3. Education programmes.
4. Youth development.
5. Humanitarian projects.
6. Community development.
7. Environmental programmes.
8. Research and advocacy.

The first demonstration will focus on three configurations: **Public Health, Health Organisations and Education**. These are demonstration priorities, not a claim that the other sectors are already implemented.

## Shared capabilities

- Organisational data hub for spreadsheets, documents and programme records.
- Programme and activity management.
- Monitoring indicators, targets and progress.
- AI-assisted document understanding and organisational questions.
- Analytics and performance summaries.
- Evidence-based report drafting.
- Organisation settings, user roles and access controls.

## Demonstration scope

The demonstration will show three shared product capabilities across the three initial sector scenarios:

1. **Data Hub:** import and review synthetic programme information.
2. **Programme Monitoring:** create programmes, activities, indicators and targets; record progress.
3. **AI Intelligence and Reporting:** ask questions about the available records, identify gaps, summarise performance and prepare a draft report with source references.

The demonstration is a limited proof of concept. It is not a complete MVP or production-ready organisational information system.

## Product principles

1. One shared platform; configurable sector experiences.
2. Simple workflows and clear language; avoid unnecessary complexity.
3. Work with existing organisational information where practical instead of forcing immediate replacement of every system.
4. Keep calculations and indicator values deterministic and traceable.
5. AI may extract, classify, summarise and explain; it must not invent results.
6. Show where each important figure or statement came from.
7. Protect sensitive personal and health information through data minimisation and access controls.
8. Be explicit about what is conceptual, demonstrated, tested and deployed.

## Problem hypothesis and validation

The working hypothesis is that some organisations struggle to understand programme performance because relevant information is spread across spreadsheets, documents, forms and separate applications. The platform may reduce manual consolidation and improve the traceability of monitoring and reporting.

This is a hypothesis to validate with real users. Existing platforms already offer programme management, data collection, monitoring and reporting capabilities. The project must research those alternatives and identify a specific unmet need before making claims of uniqueness.

## Current limitations

- No live organisational data.
- No field research or user interviews claimed.
- No production integrations.
- No clinical decision support or patient-management functionality.
- No complete security assessment.
- No deployed multi-tenant service.
- The initial demonstration will use synthetic data.

## Intended public-interest value

Explore how accessible, configurable and responsible AI-supported tools could help organisations make better use of their programme information, monitor delivery and communicate results accurately.
