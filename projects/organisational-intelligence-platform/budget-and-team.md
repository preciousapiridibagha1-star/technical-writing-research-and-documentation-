# Indicative Development Budget and Team

## 1. Budget context

The figures below are an initial planning allocation for a potential USD 25,000 project budget. They are not vendor quotations, confirmed costs or a commitment to spend the full amount in these proportions.

The budget is intended to support a focused demonstration and the necessary research, technical work, testing and documentation. It should not be presented as enough to deliver the entire long-term platform across all sectors.

## 2. Illustrative allocation

| Work area | Planning amount |
|---|---:|
| Backend and platform engineering | $6,000 |
| Frontend and user interface | $3,000 |
| AI integration and document intelligence | $3,000 |
| Data architecture and analytics | $2,500 |
| Security and privacy implementation/review | $2,000 |
| Testing and quality assurance | $2,000 |
| User research and pilot testing | $1,500 |
| Cloud infrastructure and software services | $1,000 |
| Technical documentation and deployment | $1,000 |

| **Total** | **$25,000** |

These amounts must be revised after the requirements are validated and qualified collaborators provide estimates. Actual fellowship rules and eligible expense categories must also be checked before finalising a budget.

## 3. Likely collaboration needs

- **Project lead:** problem definition, research, product decisions, requirements, coordination and reporting.
- **Frontend developer:** dashboard, forms, tables, upload experience and report interface.
- **Backend developer:** data model, authentication, programme records, imports and APIs.
- **AI engineer or experienced full-stack developer:** document processing, retrieval-grounded question answering and report drafting.
- **Security/privacy adviser:** review data flows, access controls and handling of sensitive records.
- **Testers or sector advisers:** review whether workflows and indicators make sense in real organisational contexts.

Some roles may be combined depending on collaborator experience and the final scope.

## 4. Cost-control principles

- Build only the three demonstration workflows first.
- Use synthetic data.
- Prefer established, maintainable tools over unnecessary custom infrastructure.
- Avoid expensive integrations before user need is established.
- Do not build a separate application for every sector.
- Keep the shared platform small and configurable.
- Reserve time and budget for testing, privacy and documentation.

## 5. Funding and delivery honesty

The $25,000 figure is a proposed project-budget ceiling for planning purposes, not personal income and not a confirmed development award.

The project should be scoped so that the demonstration can be delivered responsibly within available resources. Any expansion into a production platform will require further technical planning, validation and funding.
