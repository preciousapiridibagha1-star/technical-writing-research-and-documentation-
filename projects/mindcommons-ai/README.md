# MindCommons AI

**AI-powered tools for community-governed mental-health data commons**

**Project status:** Research and product concept. No prototype has been built.  
**Fellowship context:** Independent project concept being prepared for the Mozilla Foundation 2027–2028 Track I Embedded Fellowship, with SRHIN as the host under consideration.  
**Relationship to SRHIN:** This proposal is not commissioned, approved, endorsed or deployed by SRHIN. It is a concept for discussion and refinement against the official host brief and SRHIN's existing work.

## What is MindCommons AI?

MindCommons AI is a proposed web application that combines role-based dashboards with a coordinated AI assistant. It is intended to help people and communities understand, govern and oversee how community-contributed mental-health data is collected, accessed, reused and connected to AI development.

The platform is designed around four connected responsibilities:

1. **Community governance and data commons** — make community-defined rules, stewardship responsibilities and benefit-sharing arrangements visible and actionable.
2. **Consent and permissions** — help participants understand specific data uses and manage their choices.
3. **Responsible data-use requests** — help researchers submit clear requests and help authorised reviewers assess them against consent, privacy and community rules.
4. **Transparency and accountability** — maintain understandable records of requests, reviews, decisions and agreed community benefits.

AI is a central assistance layer across the application. It explains, guides, summarises, checks for missing information and prepares review material. It does not replace participant choice, community authority, ethics review or accountable human approval.

## How the application will feel

MindCommons AI is not just a chatbot. It is a web application with:

- A participant centre.
- A researcher workspace.
- A governance and community oversight workspace.
- A transparency and benefit-sharing area.
- An AI assistant that is available within the user's permitted workspace.

The AI capabilities are organised by task, not by a requirement to build many separate models. One coordinated AI service may provide participant guidance, researcher assistance, governance support, transparency summaries and benefit-reporting assistance.

## Important design principles

- Communities must have real decision-making power; a dashboard alone does not create community ownership.
- Individual consent and collective/community governance are related but not interchangeable.
- AI assists people and workflows; it does not independently grant access to sensitive data or make final ethical decisions.
- The application should connect to existing systems only where SRHIN confirms a need and an approved, secure integration is possible.
- The system should use the minimum necessary data and protect private participant information.
- The first version must stay focused on the core governance journey rather than becoming a general health platform.

## What this project is not

MindCommons AI is not a mental-health chatbot, diagnostic tool, clinical screening system, electronic medical record or replacement for GENSCORE, Gencurate or BraveHeart. It will not provide clinical advice or make decisions about a person's care.

## Validation status

The product direction is a research-informed proposal, not a confirmed solution to a verified gap in SRHIN's infrastructure. Public SRHIN materials already describe consent, de-identification, access controls, clinical review and governance oversight. The next step is to validate with SRHIN and affected communities what remains difficult, missing or insufficiently supported.

No API access, internal workflow, partnership, community consultation or deployment is claimed.

## Current deliverable

The current deliverable is a coherent, evidence-linked product framework and set of diagrams in this repository. Dashboard visual mockups will be designed after the product scope and system architecture are reviewed.
