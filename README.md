## Technology Research & Technical Documentation
### Focus: AI systems, responsible technology and digital policy

I research technology problems, analyse how digital systems should work, examine their risks and implications, and turn complex information into clear technical documentation and practical recommendations.

This repository is **one master portfolio**. It brings together research, systems analysis, AI governance, technical writing and documentation as connected parts of one professional direction—not as unrelated job identities.

I am building evidence through self-directed projects and research. I do not present myself as a software engineer or claim coding, production deployment, integrations, community partnerships or measured outcomes that I have not completed.

## My professional direction

**Technology Researcher & Technical Documentation Specialist**

The central work pattern is:

**Understand a technology problem → research the evidence → analyse the system and its implications → develop a clear solution or recommendation → document it so people can understand, assess or use it.**

Technical writing is not a separate side activity in this portfolio. It is how the research, system analysis and recommendations become usable.

## Evidence and honesty standard

- Research is labelled as desk research unless primary research was conducted.
- A product idea is labelled a concept.
- An architecture diagram is a proposed design, not a working system.
- A wireframe is not a prototype.
- A plan is not an implementation or test result.
- Organisational public statements are attributed; they are not treated as independent audits.
- No host endorsement, community consultation, integration, deployment or measured impact is claimed without evidence.
