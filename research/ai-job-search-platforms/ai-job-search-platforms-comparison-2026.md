# AI Job-Search Platforms: A Practical Comparison for 2026

**Research status:** Updated desk research; not a hands-on product test  
**Last checked:** 7 November 2026  
**Original comparison:** Jobright, Sonara, LoopCV and Sorce  
**Main audience:** Job seekers comparing AI tools, with a separate check needed for people applying from Nigeria

## Executive summary

AI job-search tools do different jobs. Some help find vacancies, some compare a CV with a job description, some fill in application forms, and some submit applications automatically. Calling all of these "AI auto-apply tools" hides important differences.

The original June 2026 report needs major corrections. Its prices for LoopCV are out of date, it describes Sorce as iOS-only even though a Google Play listing is now available, and it treats several company claims and online reviews as if they were independently proven facts. It also gives reliability scores without showing a clear scoring method.

This updated version removes the old winner ranking. It compares what can be checked from current product pages and official terms, and marks what still needs a real account test.

**Early practical conclusion:** Do not pay for an AI job-search tool just because it promises to send many applications. First check whether it supports your country, shows the actual jobs it wants to apply for, lets you review the answers, and provides clear prices and cancellation terms.

## 1. What the tools actually do

A job-search process has several separate steps:

1. **Job discovery:** find vacancies from company websites and job boards.
2. **Job matching:** compare the vacancy with a person's skills, experience, location and preferences.
3. **CV tailoring:** adjust the CV for a particular vacancy without inventing experience.
4. **Application preparation:** fill in forms and prepare answers or a cover letter.
5. **Application submission:** send the application to the employer.
6. **Application tracking:** record what was sent and any response.

A platform may support some steps and not others. A filled form is not necessarily a submitted application. A submission message is not proof that the employer received or reviewed it.

### Jobright

Jobright describes itself as an AI job-search copilot. Its product pages advertise job matching, fit scores, CV and cover-letter support, application autofill and career guidance.

The original report's exact figures for job database size, daily new listings and user numbers should not be repeated as established facts without a dated source and clear attribution. These are company-reported figures, not independent measures of unique, current vacancies.

**What to check before use**
- Can a user based in Nigeria create and keep an account?
- Are vacancies outside the United States available and current?
- Does the tool show the full job description and employer link?
- Does it only fill forms, or can it submit them?
- Can the user review every answer before submission?
- What is included in the free plan and what triggers payment?

Official site: https://jobright.ai/

### Sonara

Sonara's current terms identify BOLD LLC as the service operator and describe an AI service that searches for jobs matching a user's profile and preferences, then applies at the user's request. The terms describe a job queue that users can review and edit.

The terms also explain that subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in time. They state that refunds are not guaranteed after a trial has expired. They warn that employer websites may use bot detection or verification, and that Sonara cannot guarantee that an application is properly received or processed.

The old report's $2.95 trial and $23.95 per four weeks should be treated as historical or unconfirmed figures, not current public pricing. I could not confirm a current public price during this review.

**What to check before use**
- What exact amount will be charged?
- When does the trial end: by date, number of applications or whichever comes first?
- How do you cancel, and what confirmation will you receive?
- Is the service available to a person living in Nigeria?
- What personal information is shared with employers and other service providers?
- Can the user inspect the job queue before applications are sent?

Official terms: https://www.sonara.ai/terms-conditions  
Official privacy policy: https://www.sonara.ai/privacy-policy

### LoopCV

LoopCV's official pricing page currently lists:
- **Basic Looper — Free:** one job-title search loop, up to ten applications or emails per month, three job boards and up to two AI CV checks.
- **Standard Looper — €8.99/month:** up to twenty search loops, one hundred applications or emails, twenty job boards and additional CV tools.
- **Premium Looper — €29.99/month:** up to fifty loops, three hundred applications or emails, AI screening-question autofill and other features.
- **Done For You — €159.99/month:** includes the Premium features plus a weekly advisory call and additional support.

Prices and limits can change. These are the figures shown on the official page when checked on 7 November 2026. The site also describes a browser extension, CV tools, email outreach and application tracking.

**What to check before use**
- Which job boards and company career pages are included in the user's chosen country?
- Does the free plan send applications automatically or only prepare them?
- Can recruiter emails be reviewed before sending?
- How are duplicate or unsuitable roles prevented?
- Can the user pause a loop immediately?
- What is the actual cost after currency conversion and any payment fees?

Official pricing: https://www.loopcv.pro/pricing/  
Official site: https://www.loopcv.pro/

### Sorce

Sorce uses a swipe-style interface for job discovery and AI-supported applications. Its official pages describe a free tier with a daily swipe limit, tailored application materials and application tracking. The company publishes its own user and application totals; these should be labelled as company-reported, not independently verified outcomes.

The earlier report said Sorce was iOS-only and Android was still coming. That is now out of date: a Sorce listing is available on Google Play as well as Apple's App Store. However, the company's own download page still describes iOS as the primary app experience, so feature parity should be checked.

**What to check before use**
- Can the app be downloaded and used in the user's country?
- Does the Android version support the same features as iOS?
- What does a swipe trigger: saving, preparing or actually submitting an application?
- Can the user review the tailored CV and cover letter?
- Which countries and employers are represented in the job listings?
- What are the current paid limits and credit costs?

## 4. Important checks for job seekers in Nigeria

This is a major gap in the original report. A tool can have a working website and still be unsuitable for someone living in Nigeria.

Before recommending any platform for use from Nigeria, we need to check:

1. **Account access:** Can a person with a Nigerian location and phone number register?
2. **Job coverage:** Does it show international, remote and Nigeria-eligible vacancies, not just US roles?
3. **Work eligibility:** Does it make clear when a job requires US or other country work authorisation?
4. **Application access:** Can it complete the employer's actual application process from Nigeria?
5. **Device access:** Is there a working Android app or browser option?
6. **Payment:** Can a Nigerian user pay, in what currency, and with what card or payment method?
7. **Privacy:** What CV, contact, employment and other personal information is collected and shared?
8. **Control:** Can the user check the job and every answer before anything is sent?

**Current status:** These checks have not yet been completed through live accounts. We should not claim that any of the four platforms is fully suitable for Nigerian users until this is tested.

## 5. A safer way to use AI application tools

- Keep one accurate master CV.
- Tailor the CV only when the job genuinely matches the person's experience.
- Check the employer's own careers page before applying.
- Review every generated answer. Never let a tool invent qualifications, work history or legal work status.
- Avoid sending large numbers of applications to unrelated roles.
- Keep a record of the job title, employer, link, date and application status.
- Check whether the application reached the employer's own portal.
- Read the privacy policy and cancellation terms before uploading a CV or paying.
- Start with a free plan or a small test, where available.

## 6. Research method and limitations

This is a desk-based review of official product, pricing, app-store and legal pages. It is not a controlled product test and does not measure interview or employment outcomes.

For this update:
- Official platform pages were prioritised for product descriptions and prices.
- Official legal terms were used for operator, renewal, privacy and application limitations.
- App-store listings were used to check whether mobile apps were listed.
- Company marketing claims are attributed to the company.
- Third-party reviews are not used to create a numerical reliability score.
- Information was checked on 7 November 2026; plans, features and availability may change.

The next research stage is a practical country-access test, if account creation can be done safely without paying or exposing unnecessary personal information. Until then, country suitability remains unknown.

## Final conclusion

There is no responsible universal winner based on the evidence currently available. Jobright appears to offer a broad job-search toolkit; Sonara focuses on hands-off applications; LoopCV offers configurable search loops and application automation; Sorce offers a mobile swipe-based application flow. Each has different costs, controls and risks.

For a job seeker in Nigeria, the first question is not which platform has the biggest job database. It is whether the platform can find roles the person is actually allowed to apply for, work reliably from Nigeria, show the user what it is sending, and charge a clear and affordable price. That is the standard the next round of research must meet.
