# Research Quality: Before-and-After Example

**Evidence type:** Editorial and methodological improvement based on the portfolio's AI job-search platform comparison.

## The original problem

The earlier comparison used numerical reliability scores and broad recommendations without a sufficiently repeatable scoring method. It also risked treating public product claims as proof of performance and did not clearly establish whether a Nigeria-based applicant could use every feature.

A polished table can still create false confidence when its evidence is weak.

## Before

> Platform A is the most reliable option, scoring 9/10. It has millions of users and automates applications, so it is the best choice for international applicants.

Problems:
- “Most reliable” is not defined or measured.
- The score cannot be reproduced.
- A company's user count does not prove individual outcomes.
- “International applicants” is too broad.
- No distinction is made between finding a job and being eligible to apply.

## After

> Public product information describes Platform A as offering job matching and application assistance. The reviewed pages do not establish that every vacancy accepts applicants based in Nigeria, and no live account or application test was conducted. Treat country eligibility and application accuracy as unverified until checked against the employer's own advert and application system.

Why this is stronger:
- It identifies the source type: public product information.
- It separates a feature claim from an independently tested result.
- It names the relevant geographic uncertainty.
- It explains what the research did not test.
- It gives the reader a concrete verification step.

## Reusable research check

Before publishing a comparison, ask:

1. What exact question is the comparison answering?
2. Is the geography and timeframe clear?
3. Which claims come from primary sources?
4. Which claims are company marketing statements?
5. What did I personally test, if anything?
6. Are scores supported by a repeatable method?
7. What important unknowns remain?
8. What should a reader verify before acting?

## Evidence boundary

This sample demonstrates research editing and methodological judgement. It does not claim that a new hands-on platform test was conducted. The full platform comparison remains desk research until live tests are completed and documented.
