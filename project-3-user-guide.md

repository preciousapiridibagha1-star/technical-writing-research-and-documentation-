# Project 3: User Guide - Cloud-Based AI Assistant

##  Introduction
This user guide outlines the core architecture, target audience, and step-by-step usage framework for cloud-based artificial intelligence assistants. Designed for non-technical users and professionals alike, it provides foundational instructions for optimizing everyday productivity, research, and content generation.

---

##  Core Capabilities
* **Conversational AI:** Natural-language interaction without requiring coding knowledge.
* **Content Generation:** Automated drafting of reports, emails, articles, and proposals.
* **Research & Summarization:** Rapid document summarization and topic exploration.
* **Writing & Editing Support:** Grammar correction, tone adjustment, and clarity enhancement.

---

##  Target Audience
* **Students & Educators:** Generating study guides and learning resources.
* **Business Professionals:** Drafting emails and organizing workplace reports.
* **Entrepreneurs:** Market research and business strategy brainstorming.
* **Writers & Researchers:** Content outlining, data organization, and fact-checking.

---

##  Step-by-Step Usage Framework

### Step 1: Access the Platform
Navigate to the web application via a supported browser and sign in to your account.

### Step 2: Formulate Your Instruction
Locate the message input field and enter a clear, natural-language prompt specifying your objective (e.g., *"Draft a professional summary of the attached notes"*).

### Step 3: Review and Refine
Evaluate the generated output, verify critical facts independently, and request adjustments or constraints if necessary.

---

##  Responsible Use & Limitations
* AI models may occasionally produce inaccuracies or outdated information.
* Users are fully responsible for reviewing and verifying generated outputs.
* Avoid inputting sensitive personal or confidential enterprise data.
