## How to read the evidence

Each area identifies:
- The problem or question.
- The work produced.
- A practical before-and-after improvement.
- What the work demonstrates.
- What remains unverified or needs further evidence.

**Evidence rule:** a specification is not an implementation; a test plan is not a test result; desk research is not hands-on testing; a proposed policy safeguard is not proof of compliance. Claims will be upgraded only when supporting evidence exists.
