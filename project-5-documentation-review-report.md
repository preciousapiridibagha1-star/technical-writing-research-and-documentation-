##  Key Audit Findings & Identified Improvements

### 1. Visual Hierarchy & Heading Consistency
Enforced a strict hierarchical standard across all files (single `#` for titles, `##` for primary sections, and `###` for sub-categories) to prevent cognitive overload for readers scanning the repository on mobile or desktop.

### 2. Reduction of Conversational Fluff
Purged extraneous introductory phrases across all documents, replacing them with direct, action-oriented imperatives and bulleted technical summaries optimized for rapid reading.

### 3. Accessibility for Non-Coding Stakeholders
Audited technical guides to ensure complex developer terminology (such as terminal commands or local environment setups) was paired with intuitive, no-code alternatives, maximizing accessibility for general users and executive assistants.
