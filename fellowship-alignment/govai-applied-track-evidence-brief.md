# GovAI Applied Track — Evidence and Fit Brief

**Purpose:** Select existing portfolio evidence for the GovAI UK Summer Fellowship 2027 Applied Track. This is an application-planning document, not a claim of professional employment or deployed product delivery.

## What the portfolio can demonstrate now

### 1. Research and judgement
The November 2026 AI job-search platform comparison shows a structured approach to a practical problem:
- identifies errors and unsupported claims in an earlier report;
- checks official product pages and terms;
- distinguishes company-published claims from independently verified facts;
- evaluates Nigeria-specific access rather than assuming international availability;
- removes numerical reliability scores where the method cannot support them;
- states what desk research cannot establish.

**Applied relevance:** evidence gathering, prioritisation, evaluating uncertainty and communicating a decision without overstating confidence.

### 2. Product and systems thinking
Vantage Automata documents a bounded task-approval concept, including problem framing, requirements, architecture, workflow, risk register and proposed tests.

Jarvis AI documents a broader business-systems concept while narrowing the first proposed MVP to one customer-support workflow.

**Applied relevance:** turning a broad problem into a scoped project, defining requirements, documenting trade-offs and preparing work for technical collaboration.

### 3. Responsible AI and human oversight
The two concepts now make explicit that:
- model outputs are proposals, not authority;
- human approval applies to a specific action or response;
- uncertain or out-of-scope cases should stop or escalate;
- permissions should be limited;
- sensitive or consequential actions should not be automated by default;
- proposed controls require implementation and testing before they can be claimed as effective.

The AI-assisted job-application analysis applies similar reasoning to truthful self-representation, eligibility checks, privacy and human review.

**Applied relevance:** identifying risks early, designing proportionate safeguards and keeping accountability with the appropriate human decision-maker.

### 4. Technical communication
The portfolio includes product overviews, structured project documentation, workflow descriptions, risk tables, proposed acceptance criteria, FAQs and user-facing guidance.

**Applied relevance:** making complex work understandable to colleagues, reviewers and non-specialist stakeholders.

## Current evidence gaps

The portfolio does not yet demonstrate:
- a deployed Vantage or Jarvis product;
- production software engineering or verified integrations;
- externally commissioned delivery;
- measured user outcomes;
- a completed project with a partner organisation;
- a merged open-source contribution.

These are real gaps. The application should not disguise them.

## How to present the experience

A truthful framing is:

> My experience is self-directed and evidence-led. I have developed research and documentation projects that move from problem framing to source evaluation, requirements, system design and risk analysis. My work on AI-assisted workflows focuses on keeping human authority clear, defining what a system must not do, and documenting how proposed controls could be tested. I am looking to apply these strengths in a collaborative setting where I can contribute to practical project delivery and learn from experienced colleagues.

This wording should be adapted to the actual application questions and supported with links to the work samples.

## Priority next evidence

1. Obtain a real review or collaboration outcome through an open-source contribution.
2. Complete one small, inspectable implementation milestone with tests, if technically feasible and genuinely completed.
3. Keep a record of decisions, feedback and revisions so the application can describe actual delivery rather than intention.

## Application rule

Choose evidence that directly answers each question. Describe the work as self-directed where it was self-directed. Distinguish a proposed control from a tested control, and a design from a deployed system.
