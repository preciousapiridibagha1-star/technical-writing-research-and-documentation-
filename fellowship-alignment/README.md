# Fellowship Alignment: One Portfolio, Eight Opportunities

**Research checked:** 7 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Show how the same honest portfolio can support the eight opportunities in the master plan. This is an evidence map, not a claim that the author is eligible or will be selected.

## Important point

We are not creating eight different portfolios. We are building one strong body of work and choosing the most relevant examples for each application.

The portfolio currently shows three main kinds of work:
- **AI systems thinking:** Vantage Automata and Jarvis AI, both clearly labelled as self-directed concepts with proposed designs, not deployed products.
- **Research and analysis:** the AI job-search platform comparison and its correction record; The Talent Paradox research still needs its full source and content review.
- **Technical writing:** guides, FAQs, documentation reviews and before-and-after examples.

## Priority order for portfolio development

This is a portfolio-building order, not a prediction of acceptance:

1. **GovAI Applied Track:** strengthen evidence of practical judgement, clear communication, project organisation and AI governance awareness.
2. **Internet Society:** identify a real, local internet-related problem and a practical project that could benefit a community.
3. **Mozilla:** turn one narrow project into a working, tested result with a clear public benefit and real users.
4. **Tarbell:** keep it as an ambitious option, but build independent journalism ability. Any Tarbell application writing must be written by the applicant without AI drafting or rewriting.
5. **Outreachy:** make a real contribution to a suitable open-source project and record the actual review/merge status.
6. **NetHope:** build a careful research sample on responsible AI in humanitarian or nonprofit settings.
7. **ERA:AI and PIBBSS:** develop a focused AI safety research question and stronger research methods before the next call.

## What the portfolio can and cannot prove today

### It can show
- The author can structure a complex idea into clear documents.
- The author considers risks, human review and system boundaries.
- The author can compare technology options and explain uncertainty.
- The author can improve documentation and correct unsupported claims.
- The author has self-directed project concepts with a documented direction.

### It cannot yet show
- A working, deployed Vantage or Jarvis system.
- A verified AI integration or successful end-to-end test.
- Independent software engineering or automation implementation.
- A merged open-source contribution.
- Conducted interviews, community partnerships or measured impact.
- Professional journalism or frontier AI safety research experience.

## Final note

The strongest application will not be the one that uses the biggest words. It will be the one that shows a real problem, explains the work clearly, gives evidence, admits what is not finished and connects the applicant's contribution to the programme's purpose.
