## Portfolio positioning

**Applied Technology Research & Documentation**

The portfolio presents self-directed work in AI systems, responsible technology, research, internet governance and digital policy, technical writing and project delivery. It distinguishes concepts, desk research, local implementation, submitted contributions and verified test evidence. It does not claim client delivery, formal professional certification or deployed AI products where those do not exist.

## Core capability areas

1. **AI Systems & Practical Implementation** — Vantage Automata's bounded local proof of concept, Jarvis AI's documented concept and architecture analysis.
2. **AI Governance & Responsible Technology** — recruitment governance analysis, human oversight and privacy/accountability design.
3. **Research & Analytical Writing** — Nigeria-focused research, AI platform comparisons, claim audits and research methodology.
4. **Internet Governance & Digital Policy** — Nigeria-focused meaningful-access and digital-policy analysis.
5. **Technical Writing & Documentation** — product overview, FAQ, user guide, research brief, README guidance and documentation review.
6. **Project & Programme Delivery** — scope, requirements, workflows, risks, acceptance criteria and test evidence.

Open-source contribution is maintained as a separate external-evidence record, not a seventh capability page in the six-area Master Portfolio.

## Project boundaries

The projects are related by method, not merged into one product:

- Vantage Automata: local deterministic reminder-approval proof of concept; no AI model or external service integration.
- Jarvis AI: proposed business-system ecosystem; concept and documentation only.
- Organisational Intelligence Platform: separate organisational programme-data and reporting concept with a limited synthetic-data backend proof of concept.
- MindCommons AI: separate community data-use accountability design hypothesis; desk research and specification only.
- Cloud Infrastructure & Open Source: technical overviews and research notes.
- Architecture & Implementation: shared explanatory guide for Vantage and Jarvis, not a separate product.

## Quality and maintenance rules

- Every substantial project folder has a `README.md` that explains purpose, status, contents and limitations.
- The root README links to focused indexes rather than duplicating every project document.
- Folder-level indexes use relative links and direct readers to detailed files.
- Keep one authoritative current version of each active document; identify historical versions clearly.
- Preserve project folders and evidence history unless a change is explicitly reviewed.
- Update status pages when evidence changes; a plan is not a completed deliverable.
- Verify file paths, relative links, dates and claims before marking work complete.
- Keep the default branch as `main`; use a working branch only when review or isolation is needed.
- Do not merge old divergent branches wholesale. Compare them with main and recover only a specific, verified missing improvement.
- Do not delete or rewrite substantive evidence merely to simplify navigation.

## Completion rule

A portfolio section is ready to present when its entry-point README explains what it is, its files are discoverable through an index, its claims match the evidence, its limitations are visible and its links resolve. The [Repository Guide](REPOSITORY-GUIDE.md) is the authoritative detailed map of where files are located.
