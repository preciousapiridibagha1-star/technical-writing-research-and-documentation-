# Portfolio Demonstration Video Briefs

These are concise production scripts for short AI-generated visual explainers. They describe proposed systems, not operational products. Any generated video must retain the on-screen label **Concept design — not yet implemented**.

## Vantage Automata — 20-second explainer

**Format:** 9:16 or 16:9, clean professional motion graphics, restrained interface animation, no fake product screenshots or claims of live integrations.

| Time | Visual direction | Voice-over / on-screen message |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sec | A person types a task into a clean digital interface. | “What if AI could help organise your tasks without taking control away from you?” |
| 4–8 sec | The instruction becomes a structured action proposal. | “Vantage Automata turns an instruction into a clear action proposal.” |
| 8–13 sec | A review panel displays the proposed action with Approve and Reject choices. | “You review the details and decide what happens next.” |
| 13–17 sec | Reject stops the flow; approve moves to a clearly marked mock action. | “No approval, no action. Human control stays at the centre.” |
| 17–20 sec | Project name and concept-design label. | “Vantage Automata. A human-supervised AI task system, designed for a future rebuild.” |

**Accuracy notes:** Do not show real integrations, live customer accounts, autonomous execution, production dashboards or claims that the product is available. The approved path should be shown as a proposed flow or mock demonstration only.

## Jarvis AI — 20-second explainer

**Format:** 9:16 or 16:9, clear business-system animation, simple labelled layers, no implication that the agents are currently running.

| Time | Visual direction | Voice-over / on-screen message |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sec | A business owner faces several disconnected business tasks. | “Running a business means managing many moving parts.” |
| 4–8 sec | A central lead-agent concept connects to a small customer-support workflow. | “Jarvis AI explores how specialised AI assistance could support business operations.” |
| 8–13 sec | A customer question is matched to approved business information and a draft response appears. | “Its first proposed use case is a customer-enquiry assistant grounded in approved information.” |
| 13–17 sec | A human reviewer checks and approves or edits the draft. | “A person reviews every response. The system does not act on its own.” |
| 17–20 sec | Wider ecosystem diagram fades in behind the MVP, labelled future direction. | “Start with one useful workflow. Expand only when the need is proven.” |

**Accuracy notes:** Show the customer-enquiry assistant as the proposed first MVP. Label the wider multi-agent ecosystem as a future architecture direction. Do not depict deployed agents, real customer integrations, measured business results or autonomous operations.

## Shared visual and production rules

- Use the original project diagrams as reference assets once they have been uploaded and checked.
- Keep terminology consistent with the written project documentation.
- Avoid fake metrics, customer logos, testimonials, revenue claims or fabricated screenshots.
- Add a visible final-frame note: **Self-directed concept. Proposed design; implementation and testing not yet verified.**
- Keep the narration understandable and the visuals legible on a mobile screen.
